Interest and liquidity risk letters

Mercedes-Benz Financial Services
In production

160 hourssaved every month

  • Power Automate
  • Office Scripts
  • AI Builder
  • Teams
  • SharePoint
  • Outlook

Every month the team prepares a risk letter for each of the 40 companies in the group. Analysts opened dozens of spreadsheets, cross-checked numbers between files and wrote each letter with the results: 8 people, 2 weeks a month, working towards a deadline.

  1. ExcelRead every file
  3. Office ScriptCheck the analysts' rules
  5. AI BuilderLook into what's off, propose a fix
  7. AI BuilderWrite the letter
  9. PDF + OutlookAttach it to the email
  11. AnalystReview and click Send

a person decides AI drafts, a person reviews

  1. The workflow reads the numbers from every Excel file involved, for every company.
  2. It checks them against the same rules the analysts work with.
  3. If anything is off, AI looks into the related files and emails for more information and sends a proposed solution to the analyst.
  4. AI writes the letter, which is converted to PDF and attached to the final email for the stakeholders.
  5. A person reviews that everything is correct and clicks Send.

  1. SAP GUIExport weekly invoice report
  3. Business rulesSort into 6 categories
  5. ExcelCompare with last run
  7. OutlookEmail drafts per owner
  9. ExcelTrack follow-ups

a person decides AI drafts, a person reviews

  2. Filters are applied to the downloaded Excel file, following the team's business rules.

  4. Status tracking shows which invoices are pending, chased or confirmed.
